Cross-Modal Latent World Models

Mentored by Chuning Zhu

Goal: a robot world model that predicts only task-relevant future facts in a compact latent space, so a planner can reach natural-language goals without generating pixels or text.

world modelscontrastive learningMPPI controlCLIPPyBulletPython

What I did

  • Designed the model. A CLIP image encoder embeds the camera frame, and an MLP conditioned on a candidate action sequence predicts the CLIP text embedding of what will be true H steps later.
  • Built the training pipeline. Turned expert and random-exploration rollouts into (frame, action sequence, future frame) samples with auto-generated true/false statements, then trained the model contrastively to pick out the true one.
  • Planned with MPPI. Sampled candidate action sequences, scored each against the embedded goal sentence, and tuned the planner, raising task success from 0% to 60%.
  • Analyzed the model. Trained 3B+ parameter models; t-SNE/UMAP and linear probes showed the latents encode task facts like block contact.
