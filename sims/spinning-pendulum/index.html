Spinning Pendulum Lab

A pendulum hangs from the top-center of a merry-go-round and swings in a plane that turns with it. Below ωc = √(g/L) the bottom is a center. Above it, the bottom becomes a saddle and two new centers appear at θ* = ±arccos(g/(Lω²)).

H = v²/2 − (g/L)cos θ − (ω²/2)sin² θ is conserved when γ = 0, so every orbit stays on one contour of the phase plane. With friction, H drains away and the pendulum settles into a well.

Tr = −γ. With γ = 0 the sign of Det decides: Det > 0 is a center, Det < 0 a saddle. At ω = ωc, Det at θ = 0 passes through zero: the pitchfork.
