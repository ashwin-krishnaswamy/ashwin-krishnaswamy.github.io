Intermediate Axis Lab

Toss a book about each principal axis and watch why spins about the middle axis flip. Integrates Euler's equations and traces angular momentum on the sphere |L| = const, with linearized stability at all six equilibria.

Spinning Pendulum Lab

A pendulum swings from the top of a rotating merry-go-round. Spin it past ωc = √(g/L) and the bottom equilibrium turns into a saddle while two new stable tilts appear: a pitchfork bifurcation you can watch happen.
